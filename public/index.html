About us

Meet The Grazing Outlaw.

The Grazing Outlaw creates customizable charcuterie experiences for weddings, receptions, and special celebrations. Our goal is to make grazing feel fun, welcoming, and easy for guests while giving couples a presentation that feels special enough for the day.

From a relaxed self-serve cup bar to made-to-order premium cups and tasting bites, each package is designed around choice, presentation, and a smooth guest experience.
